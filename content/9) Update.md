> [!NOTE]  Brief
>> **Who are Impulse Buyers?**
> Why do we fill our already overflowing closets with clothes we’ll eventually get bored with?
> For many, especially between the ages of 18–25, it’s so much deeper than buying new clothes—you're often trying on (or buying into) different identities. 
> 
> This sounds harsh and maybe even a little sad, but it’s just an acknowledgement that social media often pushes us toward quick "trial and error" cycles rather than slowly cultivating our own style. But this trial-and-error that we keep defaulting to is harmful. It leads to wasted money, crowded spaces, and a lingering sense of guilt.
> 
> >**So, what am I working on and how did I get here?**
> I’m trying to make online shopping intentional, helping us define our own style and make the inspiration-to-purchase cycle our own. 
> 
> Imagine a space where a cart full of items you can't quite afford or don’t want right now transforms into a growing inspiration board. 
> 
> On the other hand, when you *do* buy items? You log images of your outfits in the same space, tracking the garment's journey as you own it. Essentially, I want to create a digital board—much like Pinterest—but entirely generated from scratch by you, recording your evolving taste. Parallely, the hope is that it’ll also inspire you to take more photos of your outfits and wear them more. 
> 
> *(This direction was informed by deep dives into online forums and interviews with family and friends. A few, prominent groups of people I've recognized throughout this process are conscious consumers with a passion for fashion)*
