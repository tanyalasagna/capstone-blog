- ==What are the causes of impulsive behaviour?==

![[Pasted image 20260218000622.png]]

**Here's a summary:**

> The map breaks down the causes of impulsive behavior into three distinct categories:
> 
>1) **Psychological Factors (Internal):** This focuses on _why_ we feel the urge. It connects impulse buying to emotional regulation (using retail therapy for mood repair) and identity construction (buying into an aesthetic to satisfy a need to belong).
>
>2) **Environmental Factors (External):** This covers the triggers that push us to buy. It includes marketing tactics (scarcity, FOMO), manipulative UX design (infinite scroll, one-click purchases), and social influence (specifically social media microtrends).
>
>3)  **Financial Factors & Facilitators:** This highlights how the barriers to buying are removed. "Buy now, pay later" programs eliminate payment friction, while Fast Fashion’s low prices and quick turnover foster a culture of disposal.
>
    Underpinning these factors is a neurobiological mechanism where the mere anticipation of acquiring something new triggers a dopamine burst, providing a temporary biological high that reinforces the behavior.
