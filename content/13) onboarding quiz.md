# Onboarding Quiz

# Design Notes

Total screens: 8 (Welcome → 4 Questions → Result → Personalization → Ready)

Tone: Warm, self-aware, slightly playful. 

Each question has 3 options, each mapping to a persona:

- T = Trend Chaser
    
- C = Comfort Buyer
    
- D = Loves Deals (Deal Chaser)
    

Final persona = whichever gets the most picks. 

Users can change their persona later in settings (I will mention this on the result screen to reduce pressure)

## Screen 1, Welcome!

Headline: Hey Tanya! Before you satisfactorily check out,

Sub-headline: Let's figure out what usually pulls you in. 

Body: 4 quick questions. No judgment. Takes under a minute.

CTA Button: Let's go →

Skip link (small, bottom): Skip for now

Screen 2, Question 1

Question: What usually starts a shopping spree for you?

Option A (T): I see an outfit or aesthetic online and need to recreate it

Option B (C): I'm bored, stressed, or just need a pick-me-up

Option C (D): I spot a sale or deal that feels too good to pass up

Screen 3, Question 2

Question: You're scrolling and see a shirt you kinda like. What's the thought that makes you add it to your cart?

Option A (T): "This is just what my closet needs right now.”

Option B (C): "I deserve a little treat. Won’t hurt to have this in my closet."

Option C (D): "It's on sale and almost sold out. I'd be stupid not to get this."

  

Screen 4, Question 3

Question: A week after a purchase, how do you usually feel?

Option A (T): The trend already moved on, and now I'm not sure it's "me."

Option B (C): It’s in my closet... It’s just kind of there.

Option C (D): I forgot I even ordered it. Or I realize I already had something similar.

Option D: None of the above

  

Screen 5, Question 4

Question: What would actually make you close a checkout tab or screen?

Option A (T): Realizing I already tried this aesthetic, and it didn't stick

Option B (C): Remembering that I often feel guilty when my package arrives

Option C (D): Recognizing just how much money I’ve spent on clothing.
