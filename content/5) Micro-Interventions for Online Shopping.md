> [!NOTE] **Idea**
> I want to design and develop (end-to-end) an intervention (possibly, web extension) that appears on shopping cart pages. The 'intervention' will be non-judgmental and won’t tell the user what to do. Instead, through [[quiz design]] (yet to research and find inspiration), it will nudge them to reflect and come to their own conclusion about whether or not to make a purchase.
> 
> I also want to look into creating an 'intervention' for when users are scrolling through TikTok Shop or Instagram Reels ads, helping them make informed decisions right at the source.
> 
**Example Interaction:** 'Looks like a great find! Wondering if this is thriftable?'
> To bring this to life, I plan to design a mascot and make the experience fun and interactive, with a strong focus on branding and microcopy.
