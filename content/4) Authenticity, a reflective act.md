### What are Social Media Microtrends??? 
>*my account*

Microtrends are niche, highly specific aesthetic fads that explode in popularity for a few weeks or months before vanishing. 

- **Drivers are TikTok/Reels and fast-fashion production cycles (Shein/Temu).
    
- Visual consumption, specific "must-have" products, and aesthetic mimicry.

### Social/Cultural Movements
>*and/or the "cultural zeitgeist"*

These are organized, collective efforts to change a specific aspect of society, culture, or policy. They are rooted in shared values, grievances, or aspirations.

- **Drivers are systemic issues, shared identity, and political/social goals.
    
- Structural change, awareness, and community building.
    
- They span years or decades and keep evolving.
